From a home canteen to full-service catering.

Green Pastures Catering Services was established in 2005 by a food enthusiast who also acts as the master chef of the business. It all started with simple home handling services from a canteen and food delivery in Angono, Rizal and nearby towns, with some occasional small catering engagements.

Due to increasing demand, the business grew into a full-blown catering service that accommodates both individual and corporate clients. Over the years, it has satisfied more than a thousand customers with its superb selection of food and excellent service — catering to weddings, debuts, kiddie parties, birthdays, business affairs, social functions, internet catering, and more.
